Scenario & Real-World HCI Problem

High-Arousal Mobile MOBA Combat (e.g., Arena of Valor / 傳說對決): During intense, split-second team fights, players experience extreme adrenaline rush and panic tapping. Accidental slips onto adjacent non-combat utility buttons (e.g., settings, scoreboard, chat wheel) disrupt basic attack combos, leading to disastrous team wipeouts and defeats.

Innovation & Fitts' Law Mechanism

Asymmetric Target Sizing & Spatial Guard-Banding: The critical ATTACK button is given a large width (W=96px) for rapid, error-tolerant tapping. Non-combat utility buttons (CFG) are downsized (W=28px or 54px) across distances (A=140px to 280px) and pushed to peripheral corners to deliberately increase ID (Index of Difficulty), creating motor friction to prevent accidental triggers.
